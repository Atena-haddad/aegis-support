# Billing, Invoices & Refund Policy (SOP-103)
- **Downloading VAT Invoices**: Workspace admins can download PDF VAT invoices under **Settings -> Billing -> Invoice History**. Reverse-charge EU VAT IDs must be entered before the 1st of the billing month.
- **Double Charges**: Duplicate Stripe webhook captures are automatically refunded within 48 hours. If an invoice shows two settled charges after 48 hours, escalate to Finance Ops with the `ch_...` transaction ID for immediate manual refund.
- **Vector Storage Overage Charges**: Pro tier includes 50 GB of vector storage. Additional storage is billed at $0.25/GB per month. Deleting unused namespaces reduces next-cycle overages immediately.
- **Seat Cancellation Refunds**: Unused annual seats cancelled within 14 days of renewal qualify for a prorated credit note; monthly plans do not receive mid-cycle refunds.
