# Routine Fast-Path Auto-Responses (SOP-106)
- **INVITE_MEMBER**: To invite a new team member, navigate to **Workspace Settings -> Members -> Invite**, enter their email, and assign either `Viewer`, `Editor`, or `Admin` role.
- **DOWNLOAD_VAT**: You can download all monthly VAT invoices directly from **Settings -> Billing -> Invoice History**. Ensure your EU VAT number is saved under Tax Details.
- **DARK_MODE**: Dark mode can be toggled anytime from the top-right profile menu under **Appearance -> Theme -> Dark**.
- **ANNUAL_BILLING**: Switching from monthly to annual billing saves 20% and can be activated under **Settings -> Billing -> Change Plan**.
